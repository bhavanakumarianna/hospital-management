Patients & visitors

Plan your visit with confidence

Everything you need to know before, during and after your time with us.

Before your visit

Bring a photo ID, insurance card, current medications and previous reports. Arrive 15 minutes early to check in.

Visiting hours

General wards 10 AM – 1 PM and 4 PM – 8 PM. Two visitors per patient. ICU visits are arranged with the care team.

Billing & insurance

We accept most major insurers and offer cashless claims. Pay bills at the front desk or call our billing team.

Medical records

Request copies of reports, discharge summaries and imaging from the records desk within 3 working days.

Parking & directions

Free patient parking on site, with drop-off at the main entrance and accessible spaces near every door.

Patient rights

You have the right to respectful care, privacy and clear information about your treatment and its costs.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Can't find what you're looking for? Call us on +1 (404) 555-0123.

How do I book an appointment?

Use the online booking form above — choose a department, doctor, date and time, then enter your details. You can also call our front desk.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes. Please call us at least 24 hours before your appointment and our team will cancel or move it for you.

What should I bring to my first visit?

A photo ID, your insurance card, a list of current medications and any previous test reports or prescriptions.

Do you accept insurance?

We work with most major insurance providers. Our insurance desk can help with cashless claims and paperwork.

What are your visiting hours?

General wards: 10:00 AM – 1:00 PM and 4:00 PM – 8:00 PM. ICU visits are arranged with the care team.
